# IPO — Hype Video Treatment (IPO.SOLANA.XYZ)

**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 24.7 s · composition `IpoHype` · render `npm run render:ipo-hype`
**World:** white / light-gray (#F6F6F4) with a slowly drifting 140 px gray grid, black type, neon green (#7CF21A / #4DB80A) accents.
**Motion language:** every scene is a designed still that breathes: slow push-in (0.1–0.12 %/frame) on key art, a slow lateral drift,
word-by-word fade-ups, and **slide transitions** (next frame slides in from the right while the previous slides out left, 12–14 frames).
**Sound:** 120 bpm soft pulse (kick + light hats), a soft whoosh and UI tick on every slide change, one hit on the opening wordmark and one on the closer.
