FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will checking my rate affect my credit score?

No. Checking your rate uses a soft credit inquiry, which is not visible to other lenders and does not affect your score. A hard inquiry is only made if you accept an offer and proceed with the loan.

How quickly can I receive my funds?

Most personal and auto loans are funded within one business day of signing. Business loans typically fund in 1–3 business days, and home loans close in 21–30 days on average.

What do I need to apply?

You'll need to be 18 or older, have a valid U.S. bank account, a verifiable source of income and a Social Security or Tax ID number. Business applicants also need at least 6 months of operating history.

Are there any fees?

Most of our loans have no origination fee and never carry prepayment penalties. If a fee applies to your specific offer, it will be clearly displayed before you sign anything.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Absolutely. You can make extra payments or pay off your full balance at any time with no penalty, and you'll save on interest by doing so.

What credit score do I need?

We work with a wide range of credit profiles. While a score of 640+ gives you access to our best rates, we regularly approve applicants with lower scores based on income and overall financial picture.
